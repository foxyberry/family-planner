# C6 Our day (build step 4) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A grown-up edits a kid's day template by dragging the times on a clock: knobs on the rim move the shared edge between two slices, a slice can become free time and free time can be filled, and "Save the day" applies the template from tomorrow.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-m2-screens-spec.md` — "C6 · Our day" (Layout, Drag rules); board C6.

## Global Constraints
- Day types are Mon–Fri, Sat, Sun only; editing Sun also changes weekday school holidays (caption).
- Times snap to 5 minutes; a slice is never shorter than 15 minutes; knobs never cross; the school block's edges are locked.
- The template stays gap-free except for slices made free on purpose; the two sleep halves (0:00–wake, bed–24:00) are one sleep across midnight and the midnight edge has no knob.
- "Save the day" saves a new `Routine` with `from` = tomorrow (`DayPlanner.template` already picks the newest one on or before a day); today keeps the old template.
- Every time shown is 12-hour with am/pm. Views only render State; all edit rules are pure Domain functions with tests.
- New parts (`ClockKnob`, the edit-mode clock) go into the gallery and snapshots first.

## Review Focus
- Drag "Dinner ends" from 7:15 pm to 7:30 pm: dinner ends and shower starts at 7:30 pm; the caption says "Shower now starts at 7:30 pm and is 30 min."
- Drag past a neighbour or below 15 minutes: the knob stops (bump haptic).
- School knobs are grey with a padlock; tapping one shows "School hours are set in School."
- Make Play free → paper with "+"; tap "+" → a new slice for exactly that span.
- Undo walks back each change to the last save; leaving with changes asks "Leave without saving?".
- Save on Tuesday → Tuesday's home clock is unchanged; Wednesday's uses the new template.
